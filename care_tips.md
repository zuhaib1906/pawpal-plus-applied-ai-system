# Pet Care Scheduling Tips

- Wait at least 30 minutes after feeding before a walk or vigorous exercise to lower the risk of bloat.
- Give oral medication with or right after a meal unless your vet says otherwise, so keep meds close to feeding time.
- Leave about 15 minutes between a walk and a meal so your pet can cool down and settle before eating.
- Schedule grooming or a bath after a walk rather than before, so a freshly cleaned coat stays clean.
- Space two walks at least a few hours apart to avoid overexertion, especially for puppies and senior pets.
- Keep medication doses spaced by the interval your vet recommends and never stack them back to back.
- Plan high-energy play or training before meals, then allow quiet rest afterward.
- Clean the litter box away from feeding times so food and waste areas stay separate.
